Travel & Public Transit FAQ

Getting to Zaandam is simple and fast using Dutch Railways (NS). Frequent trains depart Amsterdam Centraal roughly every ten minutes. Tap your contactless debit card or mobile wallet at NS gates with OVpay.

If you plan to visit multiple regional highlights, consider purchasing an Amsterdam & Region Travel Ticket (ARTT) for unlimited travel across regional trains.

Almost all hospitality and transport staff in the Zaan region speak fluent English.
