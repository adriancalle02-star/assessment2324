Scenic Bike Route Along the Zaan

Live like locals and discover the region beyond the standard bus tour itineraries. Renting bikes together is the best way to immerse yourselves in the authentic Dutch cycling lifestyle side by side.

ROUTE INFO & BIKE RENTALS

Renting city bikes at Zaandam Station and the flat, separated trail provide free and comfortable movement along the riverbanks with a dedicated couple path.

RIDE DETAILS: Distance & Terrain

Approx. 14 km round-trip | Flat, paved, and safely separated from car traffic.

Start your cycling trip by picking up standard city bikes directly from the automated rental facility at Zaandam Station. The path quickly leads northward along designated, paved cycle paths that follow the winding curves of the River Zaan. The entire trail is flat, safely separated from car traffic, and suitable even for couples who have not cycled in years.

As you pedal north toward the historic villages of Zaandijk and Wormerveer, the urban scenery gives way to wide water vistas and monumental chocolate and oil processing warehouses. These towering brick factories provide a visual reminder of the river's trading history, contrasting with the quaint wooden houses standing on the opposite bank.
