Cozy Dining & Date Night

When evening falls over the canals, celebrate your trip over candlelit dinners and drinks without falling into overpriced tourist traps. Discover relaxed, romantic spots favored by locals.

DATE NIGHT: Dining & Reserv.

Waterfront Dining, Craft Breweries, Dessert Cafés | Reserve in advance.

Head toward Dam Square and the harbor basin at the southern end of the center to discover Zaandam's culinary hub. The square is packed with independent bistros offering heated outdoor terraces that overlook the water. Here, you can enjoy a full three-course meal featuring local seasonal ingredients at a fraction of central Amsterdam dining prices.

After dinner, continue your date night by visiting one of the cozy, dimly lit pubs tucked away along the canal alleys. These intimate venues offer an extensive selection of local Zaan craft beers and Dutch ciders served in warm, candlelit settings. It is the perfect atmosphere to unwind, recount your favorite moments of the day, and soak in the evening ambiance.
